Ente Nadu.

A community-focused digital directory concept developed during my school days, intended to help residents discover skilled local service providers.

Gallery

The Ente Nadu project landing page.

Project overview

Ente Nadu was a school project built around a simple community need: making local services easier to find. The idea was to give skilled workers a digital directory where residents could discover relevant services and connect with people in their village.

The concept aimed to promote local talent and support the village micro-economy by making service providers easier to discover.

Intended impact
