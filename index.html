I am pursuing a B.Tech in Electronics and Computer Engineering at Thejus Engineering College in Thrissur. My focus lies in understanding computing as a complete continuum: from semiconductor behavior and logic gates to structured software architecture.

Rather than treating software and hardware as isolated fields, I enjoy moving between the two. Writing clean C, Python, or React code helps bring concepts to life, while studying digital electronics gives me deep respect for the physical circuits executing that code.

Currently, I am strengthening my programming foundations, working with modern web protocols, and exploring digital logic design and introductory VLSI methodologies.
